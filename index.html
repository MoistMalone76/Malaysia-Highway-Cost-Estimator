RON95/RON97/diesel prices are fetched live from data.gov.my (Ministry of Finance, updated weekly) on page load, with the last-known rate shown above as a fallback if the fetch fails. When RON95 (Subsidised) is selected, "Government subsidy" and "Total fuel value at pump" show the gap between the RM1.99/L BUDI95 rate and the unsubsidised market price — this assumes you're BUDI95-eligible (Malaysian, MyKad, within your monthly litre quota); "Total" only ever counts what you actually pay. Toll cost is modelled from plaza-to-plaza distance at a flat rate; actual PLUS, ELITE and other concessionaire tariffs vary by class and section — no public live feed exists for these, so this figure is a manually-set assumption. EV energy assumes roughly RM0.50/kWh at home, RM1.10/kWh public AC and RM1.50/kWh public DC; charging operators (TNB, Gentari, JomCharge, ChargEV, Tesla) don't publish a live pricing API either, so these are also manually-set — check your operator's posted rate before you plan around this. Toll and EV rates in this tool were last checked 9 Sep 2026. Rest area listings (R&R and Kawasan Rehat laybys) show the direction of travel, petrol brand and general food/cafe offering for that specific carriageway only — never the opposite side of the highway, and never a stop on a highway your route doesn't use. Amenities are representative of publicly documented PLUS/LLM facilities and individual tenants change over time, so treat them as a planning guide and confirm signage on the day.
